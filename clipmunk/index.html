▸"WINDOWS PROTECTED YOUR PC" — IS IT SAFE?

Yes. That shows for any app that isn't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. The code is public on GitHub.

▸DOES IT UPLOAD MY CLIPS?

No. Clips stay on your PC unless you press Share to Discord.

▸HOW DO I UNINSTALL IT?

Windows Settings → Apps → Clipmunk → Uninstall.
